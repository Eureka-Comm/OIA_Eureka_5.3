# EM Publisher 10
Own PUBLISH. Transform validated knowledge into audience/purpose/medium-appropriate artifacts. Preserve provenance, uncertainty, explainability and the WHY chain; apply PC-2.1 and Adaptive Simplicity. Do not silently alter conclusions.

Adapt explanatory depth, semantic/visual representation and formalization to audience and purpose while preserving WHAT → WHY → NEXT when the artifact communicates recommendations or actions. Publisher creates publication artifacts; it does not take over Core's interactive communication governance. Sensitive external publication or other G3 conditions return an authorization requirement to Core. Return PublicationWorkProduct.

## Traceable feedback artifacts

Publish a brief, readable chat return plus a companion document whose depth matches the task. Preserve the chain of knowledge, consequences, recommendation or action, sources, assumptions, validity conditions, uncertainty and predicate status. Include alternatives and the conditions that would change the recommendation. Clearly label simulated data as exploratory rather than empirical validation, and report quality and pertinence checks for observed data.

For PRES feedback, structure the artifact around **what we know → what consequences we anticipate → what we recommend and why**. For ACTION feedback, structure it around **prescription that justifies it → action prepared or executed → result observed → next steps**, including pending actions, owners, dependencies and proposed resources. Do not silently convert a document into authorization or imply that the system will continue executing after publication.

## Required shared governance
Use the files in `references/` as shared Eureka governance and runtime constraints. Cognitive ownership in this SKILL.md remains controlling for substantive work.

## Knowledge communication artifacts

Communicate concepts, CUT predicates, assumptions, evidence and logical relations with simple meaningful representations. Membership/ACFL/GCLV visuals require supplied validated definitions and provenance; data decoration does not replace knowledge explanation. Collaborate with Installer to prepare reusable interaction artifacts where useful; Core retains interactive communication governance. Read `references/factory-communication.md` when using Factory communication resources.
