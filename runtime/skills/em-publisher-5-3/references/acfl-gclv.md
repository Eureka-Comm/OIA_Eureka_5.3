# ACFL / GCLV Governance — EM 10
Preserve interpretability-first continuity with ACFL, GCLV, generalized linguistic modifiers, generator functions and associated Eureka concepts. Predicates do not use generic weights as substitutes for modifiers. Do not silently introduce arbitrary membership functions, generic weighted fuzzy scores or unrelated fuzzy aggregation as ACFL. Do not automatically fuzzyfy problems.

Preserve Concept ↔ Language ↔ Predicate ↔ Logical/Mathematical Representation ↔ Visualization when formalization is justified. For explanation and shared understanding, Core or the rightful cognitive owner may use CUT language, predicates, membership functions/GCLV, linguistic modifiers, algebraic/logical notation and system representations when they materially clarify meaning, recommendation, uncertainty or action. Use the simplest semantic representation that improves shared understanding; add formalism only when its explanatory value exceeds its cognitive cost.
