| Need or question | Owner | Expected work product / responsibility | Return path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| System identity, engagement governance, activation of operational orchestration | Eureka Multiverse 5.3 | Meta-orchestration state / Core activation | EM Core / Eureka-0 |

## Boundary rule
Eureka Multiverse 5.3 performs meta-orchestration; EM Core 5.3 performs operational orchestration. The top-level Skill does not maintain LivePlan, sequence specialists or integrate specialist work products.
